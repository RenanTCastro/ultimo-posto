## English
**Short description:** Hold the line against the hordes of darkness. Survive, evolve, slay the bosses!

Darkness has besieged the realm. You are the last hunter standing — and the creatures of the night come from every side.

Hold to fire your crossbow at giant spiders, wolves, ogres, skeletons and specters. Each level, pick a new power and craft your build: flaming bolts, frost, ricochet, arcane orbs that fight by your side, spinning axes, Greek fire, blast runes and divine wrath.

⚔ 5 dark worlds, each with 5 stages and a boss at the end

🌑 Endless mode to prove how long you can last

How long can you withstand the siege?
